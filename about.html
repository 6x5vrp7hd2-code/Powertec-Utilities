ABOUT

POWERTEC UTILITIES LTD

High Voltage expertise built on operational experience.

Powertec Utilities Ltd was established in 2012 to provide specialist High Voltage engineering and operational support to the electricity distribution and infrastructure sectors.

Our experience is founded on extensive involvement with UK Distribution Network Operators and their contractor networks, encompassing overhead and underground distribution systems, substations, HV switching, network operations, construction, maintenance, commissioning and restoration.

Powertec has subsequently expanded into major critical infrastructure and European hyperscale data centre commissioning, applying established HV operational and safety principles within complex mission-critical environments.

OUR APPROACH

Competence, control and clear communication.

High Voltage operational work depends on disciplined preparation, clear lines of responsibility and precise communication. Powertec applies established safe systems of work while integrating with client, principal contractor, commissioning and specialist trade teams.

Plan before operation

Review system status, work scope, switching requirements, interfaces and safety boundaries before activity begins.

Control electrical risk

Maintain appropriate operational control, safety documentation, isolation, earthing and access arrangements throughout the work.

Coordinate across disciplines

Support testing and commissioning by arranging the electrical system safely around the requirements of commissioning teams and specialist vendors.

Verify readiness

Support inspections, plant-status checks and pre-energisation readiness before controlled energisation and handover.
