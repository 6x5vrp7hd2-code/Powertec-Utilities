OUR STORY

Built on the network.

Powertec Utilities Ltd was established in 2012 to provide specialist High Voltage engineering and operational support to the electricity and infrastructure sectors.

Our foundations are in UK electricity distribution, with experience developed across multiple DNO environments encompassing network operations, construction, refurbishment, switching, commissioning, fault restoration and project delivery.

Today, that experience extends into European mission-critical infrastructure, supporting complex data-centre commissioning and energisation programmes where electrical safety, system resilience and operational control are fundamental to successful delivery.

We create the controlled environment in which complex electrical systems can be tested, commissioned, energised and handed over safely.

OPERATIONAL EXPERIENCE

Experience developed in the field.
