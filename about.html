ABOUT

Powertec Utilities Ltd

Powertec Utilities Ltd was established in 2012 to provide specialist High Voltage engineering and operational support to the electricity distribution and infrastructure sectors.

The company's experience is founded on extensive involvement with UK Distribution Network Operators and their contractor networks, encompassing overhead and underground distribution systems, substations, HV switching, network operations, construction, maintenance and commissioning.

Powertec has subsequently expanded into critical infrastructure and European data centre commissioning, applying established HV operational and safety principles within complex mission-critical environments.
