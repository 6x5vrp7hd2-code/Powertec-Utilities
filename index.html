POWERTEC UTILITIES LTD

Specialist HV operational support for complex electrical infrastructure.

Powertec Utilities Ltd provides High Voltage engineering, Senior Authorised Person (SAP) and electrical safety support across electricity distribution, major infrastructure and European data centre projects.

Our work centres on safe system control, disciplined switching, electrical safety documentation, commissioning support and controlled energisation.

SERVICES

Operational expertise where safety and control are critical.

Focused support for HV systems from safe isolation and switching through testing, commissioning and energisation.

01

Senior Authorised Person Services

Operational control of HV systems, including switching, isolation, earthing, safety documentation and safe access arrangements.

02

HV Switching & Safety

Preparation, review, approval and execution of switching schedules, together with proving dead, application of earths and restoration.

03

Commissioning & Energisation

Operational support for testing, commissioning, load-bank activities and phased energisation of HV/LV systems and critical power infrastructure.

04

Electrical Safety Management

Control of electrical risk, permits, safe working boundaries, plant status and coordination of contractors and specialist testing teams.
